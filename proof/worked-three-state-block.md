# An eight-block group in the three-state witness

A pair of fair reset probabilities starts each block with equal mass in S and R. The weighted transition from S to R replaces some of the probability that would otherwise leave the live states. Under G the two live masses stay equal after every a-step, and at length three each is 4/27.

The separating word contains eight copies of a³# in its third group. Under G both the priority 1 and the priority 2 expected counts are 32/27. Under U they are 65/54 and 4/27 respectively. The bars in the explorer show expected occurrences in a single group. Under U the priority 2 expectations across all groups sum to one and the priority 1 expectations have a divergent sum. The exact block proof uses these sums with independence.
