# Worked group at block length three

The separating word uses eight copies of a³# in the n=3 group. A preceding # sends the run to S or R with probability 1/2 each. A priority 1 output requires the R branch and three R self-loops, giving probability 1/16 for each block under either resolver.

A priority 2 output requires the S branch and three S self-loops. Under G the probability in one block is 1/16. Under U it is 1/54. Multiplying by eight blocks gives expected group counts of 1/2 for priority 1 in either case, and 1/2 under G versus 4/27 under U for priority 2.

The rotating bars and explorer represent these expectations. The uniform priority 2 group count is generally (2/3)ⁿ/2, whose sum over positive n is one. The uniform priority 1 group count stays at 1/2, whose sum diverges. Independence and the Borel–Cantelli lemmas give almost-sure rejection by U.
