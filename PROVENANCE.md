# Provenance

Henzinger, Prakash and Thejaswini posed the distribution question in §6 of their MFCS 2025 paper. Version 1.0.0 presents the four-state construction and traces the qualitative implication to earlier probabilistic automata work. The present revision preserves that proof and adds the three-state construction. [Original question](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2025.57) · [Original version](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24572568).
