# Claim scope

The parity condition uses the largest priority occurring infinitely often. The entire input word is fixed independently of the sampled run. Both memoryless distributions have full support on the same transitions. The uniform one almost surely fails a fixed accepted input.

The qualitative answer is an implication of earlier results. The witness makes no minimum-size or priority claim. For a fixed ultimately periodic word and finite memory, almost-sure acceptance stays support-invariant. The finite replay checks explicit block identities, and the paper proves the infinite-word claim.
