# Claim scope

The complete input word is fixed independently of the sampled run. The parity rule uses the largest transition priority seen infinitely often. Each of the three-state and four-state constructions uses two letters and two memoryless full-support distributions that differ at just one row. The weighted one almost surely accepts every fixed input and the uniform one rejects the specified input almost surely.

Earlier probabilistic automata results imply the qualitative separation. This revision reduces the explicit witness from four states to three and preserves the original proof. Priority and state minimality remain undetermined. An adaptive letter player changes the quantifiers. Ultimately periodic inputs yield a finite homogeneous Markov chain after a prefix and have support-invariant probability-one acceptance.
